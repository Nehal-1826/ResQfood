How ResQfood Works

A simple, transparent process to ensure surplus food reaches those who need it most, safely and efficiently.

1. Restaurant Posts

Restaurants log in and quickly list available surplus food with quantity and expiry details.

2. NGO Requests

Verified NGOs browse live listings and request food that matches their current needs.

3. Easy Pickup

Once approved, the NGO collects the food and confirms the pickup in the dashboard.
